Marinara Sauce

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped onion
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt, divided, or to taste
  • 2 cloves garlic, pressed
  • 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes, or to taste
  • 28 ounces San Marzano tomatoes
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper, or to taste
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh basil

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Add onion and 1/2 teaspoon salt and cook until very soft and translucent, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and crushed red pepper and cook one minute longer.
  2. Add tomatoes, water, remaining salt and pepper and stir to combine. Bring mixture to a simmer and cook 10-15 minutes. Use a wooden spoon or potato masher to break up the tomatoes to very small pieces.
  3. Stir in tomato paste and fresh basil and cook 5 minutes longer.
